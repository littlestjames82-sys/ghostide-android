# Code of conduct

GhostIDE is a small studio project. The rules are
short:

- Be direct and be decent. Critique code, designs,
  and decisions as hard as you like; leave people out
  of it.
- No harassment, threats, or spam, in issues, pull
  requests, or any project space.
- Assume good faith first — most friction here is a
  missing detail, not a motive.

Reports go to gdev6145@gmail.com. The maintainer may
remove content or participants that break these
rules, and will say why.
